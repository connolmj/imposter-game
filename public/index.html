How to Play 🎮
The Goal

Everyone gets the same secret word — except the Imposter, who doesn't know what it is. Players take turns giving one-word clues. The group votes to find the Imposter, and the Imposter tries to stay hidden!

Everyone's Phone Mode

① Host creates a game and shares the 4-letter room code.

② Everyone joins on their own device.

③ Host picks categories and starts the round.

④ Each player secretly sees their word on their own phone.

One Device Mode

① Host enters all player names and picks settings.

② The phone is passed to each player in turn.

③ Hold the button to reveal your word — release to hide it.

④ After everyone's seen their word, discussion begins!

Winning

🕵️ Players win if they correctly vote out the Imposter.

🎭 Imposter wins if they avoid being caught — or if they can guess the secret word after being revealed!
